SLIDE 2


Introduction and Motivation

Source: HMC-PPT-20261007-01 | project scope, final_results.json
02


The engineering problem
Score EEG into Wake, N1, N2, N3 and REM, then turn the sequence into an interpretable sleep profile.


Why uncertainty and sequence context?
Epoch predictions can fluctuate. Evidence exposes ambiguity; training-derived transitions support coherent decoding.


Why personalized profiling?
Recording-level indicators summarize sleep patterns beyond a single accuracy score; their research validity must be checked against labels.
Academic research system; sleep profiles are not a medical diagnosis.

Project Objectives

Source: HMC-PPT-20261007-01 | checkpoint_config.json, transition_config.json, biomarker_validation.csv
03

Signal quality
checks + flags


Model A: CNN
Wake / REM / NREM


Model B: CNN
N1 / N2 / N3
Soft composition: P(Nk) = P(NREM) × P(Nk | NREM), k = 1,2,3.
Retain Wake and REM probabilities; avoid irreversible hard routing.

Research profile
+ review flag


Model C: TCN
sequence refinement


Validation-fitted
fusion + calibration

Fuse hierarchical scores with the current five-stage model as a parallel baseline.
Add the sequence model only after measuring whether the experts help.
Evaluate stage recall, macro F1, calibration and cost; no future performance is claimed.

SLIDE 12


Conclusion and Future Priorities

Source: HMC-PPT-20261007-01 | final_results.json, multistage_pipeline_proposal.md
12


What was demonstrated
One verified hybrid model and training-derived decoding: 53.75% raw → 61.99% decoded accuracy on 3,773 test epochs.


Next engineering milestone
Train coarse-stage and NREM experts; validate soft fusion against the current model. Then test a learned temporal refiner as an ablation.


What is required before stronger claims
Use grouped validation and a new locked test cohort; seek external validation and expert review of biomarker definitions and risk thresholds.
Current limits: four test recordings, historical test reuse, no clinical or external validation.
